What we learned

  • LTX is the cheap workhorse

    About 40 s and two cents per warm 5-second clip at 1280×704.

  • H3 turbo is close behind

    About 53 s per warm clip, landing on the last frame. Base 20-step took 174 s.

  • Load timing did not separate yet

    Preload demanded full GPU residency; H3 BF16 needs about 98 GB, so it failed. Loading happened inside each model’s first job instead. Fix due.

  • Video encoding costs 10 s a clip

    A fifth of each LTX clip’s time; GPU encoding should cut it.

  • Setup ate most of the bill

    171 GB of downloads took 21 minutes, plus installs and restarts. A network volume would pay off quickly.

  • Two repo bugs fixed during the run

    Placeholder LTX checksums and an outdated uv; both fixes pushed.
